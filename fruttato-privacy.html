Fruttato — Privacy Policy

Developer and publisher: Shehab Elsherif
Last updated: November 9, 2026

Fruttato is a paid, offline game developed and published by Shehab Elsherif. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

1. Data Collection

Fruttato does not collect, transmit, or share personal information or device information with the developer. It does not require an account, login, or internet connection to play.

2. Local Game Saves

Game progress, unlocked characters, settings, and other gameplay information are stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive this information. Saved data remains on your device until it is deleted through the app or device controls, or the app is uninstalled, subject to your device's operating system and backup settings. Because the developer does not receive or store these records, the developer cannot retrieve or delete a remote copy of them.

3. Advertising and Analytics

Fruttato does not include advertising, analytics, user-tracking tools, or crash-reporting services that send gameplay or personal data to the developer.

4. Purchase Processing

Fruttato may be purchased through Google Play. Google handles purchases, payments, and associated platform data according to Google's own terms and privacy policies. The developer does not directly collect or store your payment card information through the game.

5. Sharing and Security

The game does not transmit your locally stored gameplay data to the developer or third-party services. Your device's own security and backup arrangements govern the protection of locally stored game data.

6. Children's Privacy

Fruttato does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

7. Changes to This Policy

If the game's functionality or data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated and the revision date above will be changed.
